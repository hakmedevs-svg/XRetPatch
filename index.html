About

Built for advanced Android customization

What is XRetPatch?

XRetPatch is a project focused on modern Android system customization and root management. It explores the balance between flexibility, device compatibility, and maintainable architecture.

Why it matters

The aim is to provide a flexible, extensible framework for advanced users who want deeper control over Android devices while keeping the project community-driven and open for future improvements.

Requirements

Requirements and installation notes

System requirements

  • A compatible Android device
  • A supported Android kernel and firmware
  • Boot image access, where required
  • ADB and other development tools, if applicable

Important warning

Always back up your device data and boot image before modifying system components. Incorrect modifications may cause bootloops or data loss.
